# P0 Core Loop Product Specification

## Problem

The current child-facing loop interrupts every successful match with a full-screen two-character tracing exercise and a runtime-generated image. This turns success into additional work, breaks matching flow, introduces roughly ten-second image latency and per-play generation cost, and makes a non-essential image service a dependency of the core experience.

Learning progress is also overstated: a play session samples six pairs from a roughly twenty-word unit, but completing that session causes the word book to present every pair in the unit as learned.

## Confirmed product decisions

1. Runtime image generation is not part of the child-facing core loop.
2. A correct match receives immediate, non-blocking feedback: character combination, whole-word speech, and a short visual celebration.
3. Word cards must work as text-first cards; static images may be added later as reviewed curriculum assets.
4. The existing runtime image APIs may remain as internal content-production infrastructure but must not be called during ordinary play.
5. Tracing is removed from the per-match path. Optional post-level tracing is a later enhancement and is not required for this P0.
6. The word book may only claim that a word was practiced if the child actually matched that word.
7. Per-level practice must be stored separately from the globally deduplicated word-card collection so the same word can remain practiced in more than one built-in or custom level.
   `WordCard.levelId` becomes optional and legacy-only; new ownership and progress logic must never depend on it.
8. New rounds should favor words not yet practiced in that unit before repeating practiced words.
9. Stars and progress labels must have consistent, observable meaning: completion always earns one star; no more than two mistakes earns a second star; a perfect round with zero mistakes and zero hints earns the third star.
10. The first game must accurately explain that the player selects one character from each column to form a word and may long-press to view pinyin. The game rule must enforce cross-column matching.
11. Existing user changes, especially the in-progress deadlock replacement fix, must be preserved.

## Acceptance criteria

- Completing a correct pair does not open a modal or tracing canvas.
- Ordinary game play sends no request to `/api/generate-image`.
- A correct pair immediately produces speech and a visible `字 + 字 = 词语` confirmation.
- The matched word is saved as a usable text-first word card even when it has no image.
- The match is also recorded under the current built-in or custom level without changing a globally shared card's ownership.
- Replaying a unit prioritizes words absent from that unit's practice record until all unit words have been practiced.
- Selecting two cells in the same column cannot eliminate them and gives corrective feedback.
- Wrong attempts and hints are counted for the current round; completion stars derive from those counts rather than always showing three.
- Legacy local saves continue loading.
- The word book displays only actually collected/practiced words and does not call all words from a completed unit “learned”.
- Story and custom-word-list thresholds are consistent across screens.
- Lint, TypeScript, tests, and production build pass.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Generating or reviewing the complete 160-image curriculum set.
- Deleting the image-generation backend.
- Themed scene-building animations.
- Full spaced repetition or a parent analytics dashboard.
- New curriculum semesters or additional game modes.
